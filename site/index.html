Product blueprint - Nigerian agriculture

One digital identity for every smallholder farm.

Farm Basics is a platform blueprint that follows a smallholder farmer through land recording, crop guidance, soil science, market access, and automatic bookkeeping, so information captured once is reused everywhere.

A blueprint, not a prototype

This project is deliberately documentation-first: a full business case, product requirements, engineering design, and accessibility-led design system, ready to hand to an implementation team.

The problem: five disconnected systems

Today a smallholder farmer's information lives in five places that never talk to each other. Every visit starts from zero.

Government

Identity drives that stop at the office door.

NGOs

Extension visits that leave no durable record.

Buyers

Informal produce purchases with no receipts or history.

Input dealers

Unregulated advice on seed and chemicals.

Community

Agronomy passed by word of mouth only.

One platform, thirteen domains

Every domain shares one identity, one geography model, one trust profile, one review engine, and one audit trail.

Identity Field boundary recording Crop lifecycle Land history Soil advice and labs Pest and disease outbreaks Knowledge bank Certifications Partners and vendors Marketplace and escrow Automatic bookkeeping Admin and operations Audit trail

Designed for the field, not the boardroom

The design system makes hard accessibility choices on purpose: outdoors in sunlight, on a 1GB Android device, sometimes read aloud to a farmer who does not read.

Typography

AaBbCc 123 - Atkinson Hyperlegible

Chosen for its differentiated letterforms, so characters that look alike to a low-vision reader remain distinguishable. Body text never drops below 15px in the farmer app.

Primary text on the cream base measures a 13.1 to 1 contrast ratio, built for reading in direct sunlight.

Offline is a state, not an error

The Android app is offline-first with a local store and a sync engine, assuming 2G and intermittent connectivity.

Icon and word, always

Status is never conveyed by colour or icon alone. Every symbol is paired with a plain-language label.

Built for the lowest device

A hard floor of Android 5.0 and 1GB of RAM shapes every screen and every data decision.

An unusually complete blueprint

What comes next

Four blocking legal and operational questions are documented and tracked: escrow licensing, data erasure, authoritative geography data, and data residency. They gate launch, not design.
